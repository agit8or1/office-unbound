{{ icon('devices') }}iPhone and iPadMail, calendar, contacts, files
  1. Open this page on your iPhone or iPad and tap Download profile. Allow the download when asked.
  2. Open Settings. Tap Profile Downloaded, then Install.
  3. Type your password when iOS asks. It goes straight to your device, not into the profile.
  4. For files, install the Files app for iPhone and iPad and sign in with your organisation's address: get it on the App Store (it is published there as "OpenCloud", the open-source project Office Unbound Files is built on).
