Outlook (classic, Microsoft 365 Apps or Office 2019 and later)

  1. Open File > Add Account and type your e-mail address{% if email %} ({{ email }}){% endif %}.
  2. Outlook asks our server for its settings automatically (a standard feature called Autodiscover) and then asks for your password.
  3. If Outlook cannot find the server, tell your administrator: Autodiscover must be published at https://{{ cfg.autodiscover_host }}/autodiscover/autodiscover.xml.

"New Outlook" and the Outlook mobile apps cannot connect to servers other than Microsoft's. Use classic Outlook, Thunderbird or the built-in Windows Mail settings (IMAP) instead.

Thunderbird

  1. Add a mail account with your e-mail address: Thunderbird looks up the settings by itself. If it does not, use the manual settings further down this page.
